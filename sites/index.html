Direct answer

SolCrys Sites creates the pages that answer engines read and buyers land on. We deliver the copy and the design, built from your approved facts, and the pages can be hosted on a SolCrys subdomain or on your own domain.

Sites is scoped and quoted per project. It is separate from the SolCrys AEO subscription but designed to run with it: AEO shows which buyer questions your pages fail to answer, Sites ships the pages, and AEO reruns the same questions to show what changed.

What a Sites project delivers

A project is scoped to what you are launching and can include:

  • Homepage. Introduces the company in the words you want buyers and AI engines to repeat.
  • Category pages. Define your category in language an engine can extract and quote.
  • Product pages. State what the product is, who it is for and the proof behind each claim.
  • Supporting articles. Answer the specific questions buyers ask AI, with one question to a heading.
  • Copy and design together. We deliver both, and work with your marketing and web teams to get them live.

How a page gets built

Four steps, and the first one happens before any copy is written.

1. Measure the questions first

We run the questions your buyers ask AI engines and record how each engine answers today. That baseline is what the finished pages are compared against.

2. Turn the gaps into the outline

Questions where your brand is absent or described incorrectly become the headings and FAQ entries on the page. The page is organized around what buyers ask, in their wording.

3. Write from your Corporate Context

Every claim on the page traces back to your Corporate Context, the record of your approved facts, claims and proof. Nothing ships without your team's approval.

4. Rerun the same questions

After launch, the same set of questions (the prompt set) runs again on the same engines, so the change in mentions, citations and share of voice is measured against the baseline.

What Sites does not do

Two boundaries.

  • It does not publish without approval. Your team signs off on every page before it goes live.
  • We commit to the pages, the baseline and the remeasurement. We report exactly what moved against that baseline. We do not promise a citation or a ranking. The engine decides which sources it names.

FAQ

Do I need SolCrys AEO to use Sites?

Sites is scoped and quoted as its own project. Each project starts with a baseline measurement in SolCrys AEO, because the questions where AI engines leave your brand out or describe it incorrectly decide what the pages need to say.

Where are the pages hosted?

On a SolCrys subdomain or on your own domain. For Cornelis Networks, the redone homepage and the new category page run on cornelis.com.

Who writes the copy, and who approves it?

SolCrys writes the copy and delivers the design, grounded in your Corporate Context, the record of your approved facts, claims and proof. Nothing ships without your team's approval.

How is Sites priced?

Per project, with hosting optional. Tell us what you are launching and we will scope and quote it. SolCrys AEO is a separate monthly subscription with a free tier. See AEO plans and pricing.

How do you know the pages worked?

We record a baseline before the pages ship and rerun the same prompts on the same engines after launch, so you see the change in mentions, citations and share of voice.
