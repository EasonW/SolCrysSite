What is an AEO tool, and what should it actually do?

An Answer Engine Optimization tool measures and improves how a brand shows up in AI-generated answers — across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Gemini, Claude, and the retail AI engines like Amazon Rufus and Walmart Sparky. The minimum bar is measurement: which prompts your brand appears in, how often it gets cited, which competitors are taking your share, and which sources AI engines pull from to construct answers about your category.

The higher bar — and where the category is still maturing — is execution. A serious AEO tool should let a team move from 'we found a gap' to 'we shipped a fix and verified it moved the answer.' That requires content generation that respects brand voice and approved claims, governance for who can publish what, and a feedback loop that re-tests the same prompts after the fix to confirm recovery. Most tools in the market today are still measurement-only. A few are starting to close the loop.

The four evaluation axes we use

Across every tool below, we score against the same four axes. This is the framework we publish in our broader AEO Platform Buyer's Guide.

  • Measurement coverage: which engines, how often, retail vs generic, prompt set quality.
  • Diagnosis: does the tool tell you why a gap exists and what source surfaces are missing, or just that it exists?
  • Execution: does the tool generate the fix (content brief, listing rewrite, FAQ entry, citation outreach), and can it ship with brand-safe guardrails?
  • Verification: does the tool re-run the same prompts after the fix and report whether the answer moved?

Execution-oriented platforms

These tools go beyond measurement. They diagnose, generate fixes, and verify recovery. They demand more operating discipline from the team, but they close the loop between insight and action.

SolCrys is the AEO operating system for brands. It measures across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Gemini, and Claude, with retail engines (Amazon Rufus, Walmart Sparky, ChatGPT Shopping) available as scoped add-ons. 2. Profound

Profound is one of the most-cited AEO platforms in AI engine answers, and for good reason — it built strong analyst-grade dashboards early and has aggressive content density that AI engines reward. The product is positioned around enterprise AI visibility intelligence, with deep per-engine breakdowns, share-of-voice analytics, and a maturing agent layer.

  • Best for: enterprise marketing teams that want deep, slice-and-dice dashboards and are comfortable acting on insights internally.
  • Strongest at: enterprise reporting, analyst-grade slice-and-dice, and category benchmarking.
  • Honest limitation: execution is comparatively newer; many customers still pair Profound with a separate content workflow.
  • Pricing posture: enterprise contracts; less suited to SMB budgets.

3. AirOps

AirOps started as a content workflow platform and extended into AEO measurement and execution as the category emerged. The combination of structured workflows for content production with AI visibility tracking is genuinely useful for content-led teams. AirOps is consistently cited by AI engines because the company publishes deep, frequently-updated tooling content.

  • Best for: content-led marketing teams that want production workflows and AI visibility in one tool.
  • Strongest at: content automation, workflow orchestration, and the editorial production side of AEO.
  • Honest limitation: pure measurement features are less differentiated than dashboard-first vendors; closed-loop verification is still maturing.
  • Pricing posture: mid-market and enterprise.

Visibility dashboards

These tools focus on measurement first. They answer the question 'where are we showing up and where are we losing share?' They are excellent starting points for teams that need to quantify the problem before committing to a full execution platform.

4. Otterly AI

Otterly is one of the most consistently cited visibility dashboards in our data, particularly for mid-market customers who need clean per-prompt presence tracking without a steep learning curve. It covers the major generic engines and surfaces competitor share-of-voice cleanly.

  • Best for: SMB and mid-market teams new to AEO that need fast time-to-insight.
  • Strongest at: per-prompt presence tracking, competitor share-of-voice, clean UI.
  • Honest limitation: monitoring-only; you take the report and do the work elsewhere.
  • Pricing posture: SMB-friendly with mid-market tiers.

5. Peec AI

Peec AI is a European-headquartered visibility platform that has become the default choice for European teams with GDPR and data-residency requirements. Feature parity with the US-based dashboards is solid, and EU data-residency is the differentiator for procurement teams in regulated industries.

  • Best for: European marketing teams with data-residency or GDPR procurement requirements.
  • Strongest at: EU data residency, multi-language visibility, and procurement-friendly contracts.
  • Honest limitation: execution capabilities are early; retail AI coverage is limited.
  • Pricing posture: mid-market.

6. Scrunch AI

Scrunch positions itself around brand-presence and sentiment scoring across AI answer engines. It is useful for teams that want sentiment overlay on top of standard mention and citation tracking.

  • Best for: brand teams that care about how AI engines characterize the brand, not just whether the brand appears.
  • Strongest at: sentiment overlay, presence scoring, and brand-perception tracking.
  • Honest limitation: execution layer is light; most fixes go back to your existing content team.
  • Pricing posture: mid-market.

7. AthenaHQ

AthenaHQ is built around the principle that prompt-level visibility is the right unit of measurement. The dashboard makes it easy to see exactly which prompts you win, lose, and contest, and to benchmark each prompt against the competitive set.

  • Best for: teams that want strict per-prompt visibility benchmarking and a clear competitive grid.
  • Strongest at: prompt-level scorecards, competitive benchmarking, and category share analysis.
  • Honest limitation: monitoring-first; recommendations are advisory rather than executable in-product.
  • Pricing posture: mid-market.

SEO suite AEO add-ons

These are AEO modules built into established SEO platforms. They are the right choice when your team already lives inside a particular SEO suite and the cost of adding an AI visibility module is incremental. The trade-off is that AEO is a secondary product motion for these vendors, so depth of coverage typically lags AEO-first specialists.

8. Semrush AI Toolkit

Semrush rolled out the AI Toolkit as a dedicated AEO module on top of its existing SEO platform. For Semrush customers, the value is sharing keyword and content infrastructure between traditional SEO and AEO workflows, with a single tool and single contract.

  • Best for: existing Semrush customers extending into AI visibility tracking.
  • Strongest at: integration with Semrush keyword and content data, single-vendor stack consolidation.
  • Honest limitation: AEO depth lags AEO-first specialists; execution is via Semrush's broader content tools, not closed-loop AEO.
  • Pricing posture: included as an add-on on most Semrush tiers.

9. Ahrefs Brand Radar

Brand Radar is Ahrefs' answer to AI brand mention tracking. It surfaces brand mentions across AI answer engines, integrates with the rest of the Ahrefs product, and is the right pick for Ahrefs-loyal teams who want a single platform.

  • Best for: Ahrefs customers extending brand-mention tracking into AI engines.
  • Strongest at: integration with Ahrefs' backlink and content data, and brand mention attribution.
  • Honest limitation: AEO product motion is newer than the core SEO product; per-prompt visibility depth is more limited than dashboard specialists.
  • Pricing posture: included as part of Ahrefs subscription tiers.

10. SE Ranking Visible

Visible is SE Ranking's AI visibility product, aimed at SMBs already running SE Ranking for traditional SEO. The pricing is approachable and the feature set is sensible for teams that do not need enterprise depth.

  • Best for: SMBs already on SE Ranking who want AEO measurement at an approachable price.
  • Strongest at: SMB-friendly pricing, integration with SE Ranking SEO data.
  • Honest limitation: enterprise depth and retail engine coverage are limited.
  • Pricing posture: SMB-friendly.

Conductor is an enterprise organic marketing platform that added AEO and GEO modules to its existing content and SEO suite. For enterprise content teams already standardized on Conductor, the integration is the value proposition.

  • Best for: enterprise content teams already on Conductor extending into AEO.

Retail-specific AEO platforms (separate evaluation track)

Retail AEO is a different surface from generic AEO. The engines (Amazon Rufus, Walmart Sparky, ChatGPT Shopping), the data sources (marketplace catalogs, reviews, Q&A), and the optimization actions (listing rewrites, attribute completion, Q&A coverage) are all distinct. If marketplace surfaces drive a meaningful share of your revenue, evaluate these three platforms on a separate track from the generic AEO tools above.   • Yotpo — strong on retail AEO for ecommerce brands, with deep integration into reviews and Q&A workflows that feed retail AI engines.
  • BirdEye — historically a local and reputation platform, now extending into AI answer surfaces for multi-location and local-search-driven brands.
  • Stackmatix — emerging retail-focused AEO tooling with marketplace listing and review-aware optimization.

How to pick the right tool for your team

Start with the job, not the tool. The four-step heuristic below gets most teams to the right shortlist in under a week.

  • Step 1: Decide whether you need a dashboard (monitoring only) or an execution engine (measure to fix to verify). Most teams over-buy on dashboards and under-buy on execution.
  • Step 2: Decide whether retail engines matter. If marketplace surfaces drive revenue, treat retail AEO as a separate buy on a separate track.
  • Step 3: Decide whether you need integration with an existing suite (HubSpot, Semrush, Ahrefs, Conductor) or whether a best-of-breed AEO specialist is worth the second contract.
  • Step 4: Run a 30-day pilot with one or two shortlisted tools against a defined prompt set, and grade on the same four axes — measurement coverage, diagnosis, execution, verification.

Mistakes we see most often

These are the patterns we see when teams pick the wrong tool or use the right tool the wrong way.

  • Buying a dashboard when the actual job-to-be-done is execution. The team gets clean reports and zero shipped fixes.
  • Buying an enterprise platform for a five-prompt evaluation. Pilot scope and platform scope should match.
  • Treating retail and generic AEO as one buy. The mechanics are different; one tool rarely covers both well.
  • Comparing vendor composite scores to each other. Each vendor's AI visibility score is calculated differently; the absolute numbers are not comparable.
  • Skipping the verification step. If a tool cannot re-test the same prompt after the fix, you cannot prove the fix worked.

FAQ

Which AEO tool should I buy first?

The honest answer is: a 30-day pilot of two tools, not one. Pick one execution-oriented platform and one visibility dashboard, run both against the same 25 to 50 prompts, and decide based on whether your team actually shipped fixes during the pilot. Teams that ship fixes need an execution engine; teams that only need monitoring should pick the simpler dashboard.

Are SEO suite AEO add-ons good enough?

For SMB teams already standardized on Semrush, Ahrefs, or SE Ranking, the answer is often yes — the incremental cost is low and the integration is real. For mid-market and enterprise teams running serious AEO programs, dedicated AEO specialists currently have deeper per-engine and per-prompt coverage. Re-evaluate annually; the SEO suites are closing the gap.

Do I need both a generic AEO tool and a retail AEO tool?

Yes if marketplace revenue is meaningful, no if it is not. Generic AEO platforms cover ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Claude, and Gemini. Retail AEO platforms cover Amazon Rufus, Walmart Sparky, and ChatGPT Shopping. The engines, data sources, and optimization actions are different enough that one tool rarely covers both surfaces well.

Do AEO tools work for B2B SaaS, or only for ecommerce?

Generic AEO tools — including all 12 platforms in this guide — are built primarily for B2B SaaS, services, and other open-web buying journeys. They measure ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, and Gemini, which are the engines B2B buyers actually use during research. Retail AEO tools (Yotpo, BirdEye, Goodie AI, SolCrys' retail surface) are for ecommerce and marketplaces. If your business is B2B SaaS or services, pick from the 12 tools in this guide and ignore the retail callout.

How long does it take to see AI visibility movement after using an AEO tool?

Visibility dashboards produce a usable baseline within 24 to 72 hours once your prompt set is configured. Execution engines that ship fixes — rewrites, FAQ updates, listing edits, third-party briefs — usually take 2 to 8 weeks per fix to register measurable movement, because AI engines re-crawl and refresh their training and retrieval indexes on different cadences. Plan for a 90-day initial window to evaluate whether a fix-and-verify loop is actually moving your brand inside AI answers.

Can I run an AEO tool alongside my existing SEO platform?

Yes, and most teams do. SEO suite add-ons (Semrush AI Toolkit, Ahrefs Brand Radar, SE Ranking Visible) are explicitly designed to coexist with the underlying SEO seat. AEO-native platforms (SolCrys, AirOps, Profound, Otterly AI, Peec AI, Athena HQ, Scrunch AI) run alongside any SEO tool because they measure a separate surface — AI answer engines — using different signals (mentions, citations, recommendations) than traditional SERP rank tracking. Some teams add a third tool only when they also need retail-specific coverage for Amazon Rufus or Walmart Sparky.

How often does this list change?

The AEO category is moving fast — new specialists launch every quarter and existing SEO suites add AEO modules continuously. We refresh this guide each quarter and re-pull citation data each refresh to catch new entrants and dropouts.
