What Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit does from public pages

Semrush positions the AI Visibility Toolkit as a practical system for SEOs and marketers to understand how brands appear in AI-generated answers. Its public documentation says the toolkit helps teams benchmark AI visibility, analyze sentiment, discover prompts and topics, track daily visibility, audit technical issues that may block AI crawlers, identify competitive gaps, and produce presentation-ready reports.

The public toolkit includes:

Semrush's biggest advantage is not one feature. It is distribution and data adjacency: SEO teams already know Semrush, already work in Semrush, and can now put AI visibility next to keyword, site audit, and competitive SEO workflows.

  • **Visibility Overview:** high-level benchmark for a domain's AI Visibility Score, mentions, cited pages, citations, monthly audience, distribution by LLM, and country-level mentions.
  • **Competitor Research:** side-by-side AI visibility benchmarking against competitors, including topic and prompt gaps.
  • **Prompt Research:** research into AI prompts and topics, positioned as keyword research for AI.
  • **Brand Performance:** share of voice, sentiment, narratives, and AI-generated strategic recommendations.
  • **Prompt Tracking:** daily visibility monitoring for chosen prompts.
  • **AI Search Site Audit:** checks for technical issues that may prevent AI bots from accessing content.

What SolCrys does differently

SolCrys is not trying to become a traditional SEO suite. It focuses on the AEO operating loop:

1. Identify where AI answers miss, misstate, under-cite, or under-recommend the brand. 2. Diagnose why the gap exists. 3. Generate a governed action brief grounded in Corporate Context. 4. Ship the change through the customer's team or agency partner. 5. Re-test the same prompt set and report whether the answer moved.

That workflow is narrower than Semrush's full SEO platform. It is also deeper for teams that care about turning AI visibility into governed action and proof.

Where we agree with Semrush

We agree with Semrush on several category fundamentals:

That agreement is why Semrush is a serious competitor. The buyer question is whether they need an SEO-suite-bundled AI visibility layer, or a specialist AEO workflow layer.

  • AI visibility should live next to competitor benchmarking, not as an isolated vanity score.
  • Prompt research is becoming the AI-search equivalent of keyword research.
  • Citation and cited-page analysis matter because answer engines do not just mention brands; they lean on sources.
  • Technical accessibility still matters because blocked or weakly structured sites can reduce AI-answer visibility.
  • Daily or weekly trend data is more useful than one-off screenshots.

Where we made different bets

1. SEO suite adjacency vs specialist AEO workflow

Semrush is strongest when the buyer wants AI visibility attached to existing SEO workflows: Position Tracking, Site Audit, competitor research, keyword/prompt research, reporting, and account-level team management.

SolCrys is strongest when the buyer wants a specialist AEO workflow: answer gaps, Deep Analysis, Corporate Context, action briefs, and re-testing. Semrush helps the SEO team see more. SolCrys helps the AEO owner decide what to ship and verify whether it worked.

2. Prompt database vs customer-specific prompt discipline

Semrush's public documentation emphasizes its prompt database and large-scale AI Analysis reports. That is valuable for research and benchmarking.

SolCrys emphasizes customer-specific Golden Prompt Sets and stable measurement discipline: choose the prompts that matter to the buyer journey, keep them stable enough to measure, and re-test them after changes. Large prompt databases help identify the market. Stable prompt sets help prove movement for a specific brand.

3. Site audit vs answer-correction workflow

Semrush AI Search Site Audit is a natural extension of Semrush's technical SEO strength. It can surface access and crawlability issues that affect AI visibility.

SolCrys focuses more on the answer layer: what the AI answer says, which sources it cites, which claims are wrong or missing, and what action could improve that answer. If the problem is technical accessibility, Semrush may be the better first tool. If the problem is answer correctness, source gaps, weak comparison framing, or missing product proof, SolCrys is the better fit.

4. Recommendations vs governed action artifacts

Semrush's Brand Performance reports include AI-generated strategic recommendations. That is useful for SEO teams that already own the execution workflow.

SolCrys turns recommendations into governed action artifacts: Deep Analyses, content briefs, FAQ improvements, comparison sections, source recommendations, and re-test plans grounded in Corporate Context. The output is designed to become work, not just insight.

When you should pick Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit

Pick Semrush if several of these are true:

  • Your SEO team already works in Semrush every week.
  • You want AI visibility in the same platform as SEO site audit, competitor research, and reporting.
  • Prompt research and large-scale topic discovery matter more than specialist action workflow depth.
  • You want to compare up to four competitors and find topic/prompt gaps inside a familiar SEO tool.
  • Technical AI-search accessibility checks are a major concern.
  • Your agency already reports from Semrush and wants to add AI visibility to the same reporting stack.

When you should pick SolCrys

Pick SolCrys if several of these are true:

  • You need an AEO workflow, not another SEO dashboard.
  • Your main pain is "we see the gap, but we do not know what to ship."
  • You need Corporate Context so recommendations stay aligned with approved facts, claims, proof, and positioning.
  • You want before/after verification on the same prompt set after changes ship.
  • You are building an agency AEO service package and need client workspaces, white-label reporting, and audit-to-action delivery.
  • You sell through retail/ecommerce surfaces and need a Retail AI layer beyond general SEO-suite AI visibility.

FAQ

Is Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit cheaper than SolCrys?

Semrush lists the standalone AI Visibility Toolkit at $99/month with no free trial on its public KB page. SolCrys Starter is $29/month, Growth is $99/month, and Pro is $349/month. The comparable entry depends on the job: Semrush's $99 layer buys AI visibility inside an SEO platform; SolCrys Growth at $99 buys a specialist AEO workflow with Deep Analysis and action briefs.

Does Semrush have stronger prompt research?

For broad prompt and topic research, yes. Semrush's public pages emphasize its Prompt Database, Prompt Research, and Competitor Research. SolCrys is not trying to out-Semrush Semrush on large-scale SEO-adjacent data. SolCrys focuses on customer-specific prompt sets, action diagnosis, Corporate Context, and re-testing.

Does SolCrys replace Semrush?

Usually no. Semrush remains useful for SEO, site audit, keyword research, competitor research, and broad AI visibility benchmarking. SolCrys replaces the part where teams export insights into a meeting and still do not know what action to ship. The two can coexist if the roles are clean: Semrush for SEO + broad discovery, SolCrys for AEO action workflow.

Which is better for agencies?

If the agency already runs SEO reporting in Semrush, the AI Visibility Toolkit is a natural add-on. If the agency wants to sell AEO audits, action plans, white-label client reports, and ongoing answer-gap management, SolCrys is closer to the service package.
