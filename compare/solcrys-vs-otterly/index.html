SolCrys vs Otterly AI

Otterly AI is an AI-search visibility tracker priced for solo marketers and small teams, starting at $29/mo with daily tracking across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot. We built SolCrys as a PLG-first AEO workflow product at the same $29/mo entry point, but with the product organized around an answer-gap-to-action-brief-to-re-test loop and Corporate Context governance. The honest choice: pure tracking with strong agency tooling and Looker Studio integration, or measurement plus diagnosis plus execution plus verification in one workflow.

Direct answer

Otterly AI is a strong AI-search visibility tracker at $29/mo, with daily monitoring across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot (Google AI Mode and Gemini sold as add-ons). The product output is a dashboard plus exportable reports plus a Looker Studio connector on Standard and above. We built SolCrys at the same $29/mo entry point, but the product is the workflow loop: every detected gap routes into a Deep Analysis, a content brief, a re-test on the same prompt set, with Corporate Context as the governance layer. Pick Otterly for pure tracking with strong reporting; pick SolCrys for the loop.

Disclosure: we build SolCrys, so this is opinionated. We use Otterly's own pricing page as the source of truth for what Otterly does.

Scope of this comparison: this is a positioning-level review drawn from each platform's public marketing surface - homepages, product pages, pricing pages. We have not run both products side-by-side on the same workspace, so the comparison is between published positioning and verifiable feature claims, not between practitioner-validated outcomes. Treat it as buyer orientation, not a tested product review.

What Otterly AI does, in their own words

Otterly positions itself as a daily AI-search visibility tracker for solo marketers, small teams, and agencies. We summarize each capability from otterly.ai/pricing so the comparison is grounded in their own positioning.

  • Tracking across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot with daily refresh on all tiers.
  • AI Prompt Research Tool, Brand Visibility Index, Domain Ranking, Link Citations Analysis, and a GEO Optimization Audit are stated features on all tiers.
  • GEO URL Audits scale by tier - 1,000/mo on Lite, 5,000/mo on Standard, 10,000/mo on Premium.
  • Detailed Reports and Export are stated features; Looker Studio Connector is bundled on Standard and above.
  • Agency Partner program adds pitch workspaces, unlimited workspace management, and custom Looker Studio reporting with co-marketing benefits.
  • Enterprise tier includes SSO, custom payment, quarterly GEO Health Check, and personalized onboarding.

What SolCrys is, in our own words

SolCrys is an AEO workflow product organized around four published platform layers.

  • AI Visibility Measurement - mentions, citations, share of recommendation, competitors, sentiment, and answer accuracy at the prompt level across plan-included engines, with daily monitoring on priority prompts and rolling 7/30-day windows.
  • Corporate Context Layer - approved brand facts, claims, proof, voice, and competitor framing as the governed source for every AEO action.
  • AEO Agent - today SolCrys turns answer gaps into Deep Analyses, content briefs, comparison sections, FAQ improvements, and source recommendations. Publish-ready drafting and agent-assisted CMS shipping are being built.
  • Action-to-Result Tracking - after an action ships, we re-run the same prompt set across the same engines and report movement on citation rate, answer accuracy, and recommendation share.

Where we agree with Otterly

The places we agree shape the rest of the comparison.

  • $29 is the right entry price for solo marketers and small teams getting their first AI visibility baseline - we both land there.
  • Daily tracking and multi-engine coverage are table stakes.
  • Agency Partner programs matter - agencies need pitch workspaces and white-label reporting, regardless of whose tooling they pick.
  • Citations and source tracking are first-class objects, not a footnote on a mention.

Where we made different bets

These are the choices that explain who each product is for at the same entry price.

Dashboard vs workflow loop

Otterly is a strong AI-search dashboard - citation tracking, brand visibility index, domain ranking, detailed reports, and recommendations. The output is a report; your team takes it and decides what to do. We built SolCrys around the loop - every detected gap routes into a Deep Analysis and a structured content brief, and the same prompt set is re-tested after the fix ships. The work the team does after the dashboard is the part we built into the product.

Looker Studio out-of-the-box vs MCP read access

Otterly bundles a Looker Studio Connector on Standard and above - if BI consumption is your data pipeline, that integration is hard to beat. We provide MCP read access on paid workspaces, which integrates with assistant-driven workflows and AI agents rather than a single BI dashboard. Different bets on how teams consume the data.

European mid-market focus vs agency and retail wedge

Otterly's customer base is heavily European mid-market (xxxlutz, Opera, Avis Budget Group, A1 Telekom, Visit Denmark, and others on their pricing page). Our focus is agencies packaging AEO as a service line and brands selling on retail. Neither claim is exclusive, but the product roadmaps differ based on where each company sees pull.

When you should pick Otterly (we mean this)

If your team meets several of the criteria below, we would rather you buy what fits your job than buy us.

  • Pure tracking and reporting is your need - dashboard plus detailed reports plus Looker Studio out of the box.
  • You are European or have multi-country needs (50+ country support is a stated capability).
  • You want a clean prompt-count to price ladder (15 / 100 / 400 prompts at clearly published prices).
  • Your action layer is satisfied by recommendations (3/mo on Lite, unlimited on Standard+) without a separate brief workflow.

When you should pick SolCrys

And these are the buying signals we built SolCrys for.

  • Tracking alone is not enough - you want measurement, diagnosis, briefs, and re-test as a loop.
  • Corporate Context as a governance layer for what agents say about your brand matters.
  • You are an agency and want client workspaces and white-label reporting as dedicated tiers (Agency $1,499 / Agency Pro $4,999) rather than as an upgrade on a tracking plan.
  • You sell on retail and want Amazon Rufus, Walmart Sparky, and ChatGPT Shopping as part of the same product via the Retail AI add-on.
  • Google AI Mode and Gemini are part of your buyer journey today, and you want them included in Growth ($99/mo) without add-on charges.

FAQ

Why pay the same $29/mo for SolCrys vs Otterly Lite?

Otterly Lite buys you 15 prompts of pure tracking on ChatGPT plus Google AIO plus Perplexity plus Copilot with weekly recommendations. SolCrys Starter buys you a 20-prompt monitoring set on ChatGPT plus 2 Deep Analyses per month plus 1 Content Audit per month plus MCP read access - the building blocks of the workflow loop. The same headline price; different shape.

Does SolCrys cover Google AI Mode and Gemini?

Yes, on Growth ($99/mo) and above. Both engines are included without add-on charges. Otterly bundles them as paid add-ons ($9 to $149/mo depending on tier).

What about Looker Studio integration?

Not today. We expose MCP read access on paid workspaces; the integration layer is API-driven rather than BI-dashboard driven. If a single Looker Studio dashboard is your primary consumption surface, Otterly is the more direct fit.

Are agency plans comparable?

Different shapes. Otterly's Agency Partner program upgrades Standard or Premium with pitch workspaces, custom Looker Studio reports, and co-marketing. Our Agency $1,499 and Agency Pro $4,999 are dedicated tiers with client-org capacity, white-label reporting, and built-in action briefs - not an upgrade on a tracking plan.

Why is this comparison published by SolCrys?

Because pretending we are neutral would be worse. We do not quote Otterly's blog or research as authority - only Otterly's own pricing and product pages. If you find a factual error here, tell us and we will correct it.
