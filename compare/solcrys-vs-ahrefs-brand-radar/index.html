Direct answer

Ahrefs Brand Radar is the AI visibility product of the Ahrefs SEO platform. Its moat is an AI Visibility Index that Ahrefs sizes at 449M+ prompts on its Brand Radar page (475M+ on its pricing page), modeled from real searches, so you can see any brand's AI visibility with no setup. It also tracks YouTube, TikTok, and Reddit visibility in beta. As of Sept. 26, 2026, Brand Radar is sold standalone from $199 a month (Ahrefs' help center lists $199 per platform index and $699 for all platforms), and Custom Prompts packages start at $50. SolCrys is a governed AEO execution platform organized around the Measure → Diagnose → Execute → Verify loop with Corporate Context governance. Pick Brand Radar if your team already uses Ahrefs or if cross-brand research at index scale is your top criterion; pick SolCrys if you want the loop as a weekly rhythm.

Disclosure: we build SolCrys, so this is opinionated. We use Ahrefs' own Brand Radar, pricing, and help-center pages as the source of truth for what Brand Radar does.

Scope: this is a positioning-level review drawn from each platform's public pages. We have not run both products side by side on the same workspace, so treat it as buyer orientation, not a tested product review.

What Ahrefs Brand Radar does, in their own words

We summarize each capability from ahrefs.com/brand-radar, ahrefs.com/pricing, and the Ahrefs help center, as of Sept. 26, 2026.

  • AI Visibility Index: "See any brand's AI visibility instantly across 449M prompts. No setup." Starts at $199/mo.
  • Custom Prompts: "Track the exact questions your buyers ask… Free for every paid Ahrefs Lite plan and above," with 5 prompts on Lite; packages start at $50/mo for 2,500 checks, and each prompt uses 1 check per platform per update.
  • Coverage: AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Copilot in the index; Claude through custom prompts only, at 8 checks per update.
  • AI responses, cited pages, and search queries, plus Web Visibility and Search Demand.
  • YouTube, TikTok, and Reddit visibility, free for Lite-and-up subscribers while in beta.
  • AI traffic (Web Analytics, free) and AI bot visits (Bot Analytics, free while in beta).
  • API and MCP access to Brand Radar data on paid Ahrefs plans.

What SolCrys is, in our own words

SolCrys is a governed AEO execution platform built around one loop: Measure → Diagnose → Execute → Verify.

  • Measure: daily monitoring of mentions, citations, and competitors across the engines in your plan, plus a per-answer Recommendation Score (0–100, with every point tied to quoted answer text).
  • Diagnose: Deep Analyses for prompt, answer, citation, and source gaps; Content Audits for page answer-readiness; and Answer Accuracy, which grades AI answers against your Corporate Context.
  • Execute: approved recommendations become Action Hub tasks with an owner and a human approval step. Your team publishes in its own CMS; SolCrys has no CMS connector and never publishes on your behalf.
  • Verify: SolCrys re-runs the same frozen prompt set on the same engines, and a Content Audit re-audit shows whether a page's score recovered. Before/after movement is an observation, not proof of cause.
  • Corporate Context: approved facts, claims, proof, voice, and competitor framing, set at the organization level as the governed source for every action.

Where we agree with Ahrefs

The places we agree shape the rest of the comparison.

  • AI demand data at scale is valuable. Knowing which prompts people actually run is a real input to AEO strategy, and Ahrefs' index is modeled from real searches rather than synthetic prompts.
  • Channels beyond AI answers matter. YouTube and Reddit show up in AI answers and deserve their own coverage rather than being treated as side notes.
  • Citations and cited pages are first-class objects, not a footnote to mentions.
  • AEO belongs next to organic search performance, not in a silo. For Google's AI surfaces in particular, Google's May 2026 guide says optimizing for generative AI search is still SEO.

Where we made different bets

These are the choices that explain who each product is for.

Visibility database vs workflow loop

Brand Radar's primary value is its index plus the analytics layer over it. The output is insight; your team decides what to do. We built SolCrys around the loop: a gap routes into a Deep Analysis, an approved Action Hub task, and a fix your team ships, and then we re-run the same prompt set. Both are valid shapes; they imply different team rhythms.

SEO-suite bundle vs standalone AEO platform

Brand Radar can be bought on its own, but its pull is the Ahrefs platform around it: rank tracking, backlinks, site audits, and keyword data, with API and MCP access on paid plans. If you already use Ahrefs, that bundle is hard to beat on integration and a single bill. SolCrys is AEO-only; we don't compete on SEO-suite breadth, and we recognize the bundle argument.

Cross-channel breadth vs Corporate Context governance

Ahrefs invested in breadth (YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, Copilot) and index scale. We invested in the supply side, Corporate Context, and in the action-to-re-test loop. If "tell me everywhere my brand shows up" is the core job, Brand Radar's breadth wins. If "ground every action in approved brand facts and check whether the fix moved the answer" is the core job, Corporate Context plus our loop fits better.

Index-first vs prompt-set-first

Brand Radar's index covers prompts Ahrefs models from its keyword database; tracking your own questions uses Custom Prompts, billed in checks, with a small allowance on paid Ahrefs plans. SolCrys starts from a workspace prompt set built for your brand and category and doesn't offer a cross-brand index. Different bets on where prompt sets come from.

When you should pick Ahrefs Brand Radar (we mean this)

If your team meets several of these criteria, we'd rather you buy what fits your job than buy us.

  • Your team already lives in Ahrefs and wants AEO inside the same platform and bill.
  • Index scale is a primary criterion: you want to research any brand's AI visibility, including competitors, with no setup.
  • You want AI answers plus YouTube, TikTok, and Reddit visibility in one product.
  • You need Microsoft Copilot in the same view; SolCrys doesn't track Copilot.
  • You're comfortable taking the insight and running the action layer elsewhere.

  • You want the loop (Measure → Diagnose → Execute → Verify), not just a visibility database.
  • Corporate Context matters as a governance layer, and you want Answer Accuracy to grade AI answers against your approved facts.
  • You're a marketing team or agency that doesn't already use Ahrefs, so the bundle argument doesn't apply.
  • You sell through retail assistants: SolCrys's Retail AI add-on covers Amazon Alexa for Shopping, and Walmart Sparky coverage is delivered as a service. Brand Radar doesn't list either.
  • You're an agency packaging AEO as a service line and want client-organization capacity (Agency $1,499 or Agency Pro $4,999), with white-label reporting delivered as a service.

FAQ

Does SolCrys have a search-backed prompt database the size of Brand Radar's?

No. Brand Radar's AI Visibility Index (449M+ prompts, per Ahrefs) is a cross-brand research database, and its moat is scale. SolCrys tracks workspace prompt sets built for your brand and category, and Prompt Pulse, our separate free tool, gives relative AI-demand scores rather than an absolute-volume index. If you need to research any brand across a category instantly, pick Brand Radar; if you need to act on the prompts you track and re-test them, pick SolCrys.

If we already pay for Ahrefs, why would we add SolCrys?

If your need is "we ship fixes, re-test the same prompts, and see whether the answer moved," that is the workflow we built. Brand Radar inside Ahrefs is the cleaner answer when your need is "show me the data and let the team figure out the action."

Does Brand Radar cover retail AI engines?

Per Ahrefs, Brand Radar covers AI Overviews, AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Copilot, plus Claude for custom prompts; its help center says Grok data collection is temporarily paused. Amazon Alexa for Shopping and Walmart Sparky aren't listed. SolCrys's Retail AI add-on covers Alexa for Shopping, and Walmart Sparky coverage is delivered as a service.

Does SolCrys track Microsoft Copilot?

No. SolCrys tracks five engines: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude, with coverage set by plan. If Copilot visibility matters to your buyers, Brand Radar includes it.

Is SolCrys cheaper than Brand Radar?

At entry, it depends on what you buy. SolCrys has a free workspace and a $99 Starter plan; Brand Radar starts at $199 a month standalone, and Ahrefs Lite ($129 a month) includes 5 custom prompts. But the products do different jobs: Brand Radar sells index-scale research on any brand, and SolCrys sells a monitored prompt set with Deep Analyses and the execution loop.

Why is this comparison published by SolCrys?

Because pretending we are neutral would be worse. We don't quote Ahrefs' blog or research as authority, only Ahrefs' own Brand Radar, pricing, and help-center pages. If you find a factual error here, tell us and we will correct it.
