AEO Platform Pricing Compared (2026): Starter to Enterprise

As of September 2026, published prices for Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) platforms fall into 3 bands. Entry $29–$99/month: Otterly Lite $29, HubSpot AEO $50, Peec Starter $95, SolCrys Starter $99, Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit $99. Mid-market $189–$495/month: Otterly Standard $189, Ahrefs Brand Radar AI Visibility Index $199, Peec Pro $245, Scrunch Core $250, SolCrys Pro $399, Peec Advanced $495. Enterprise: custom-quoted (Profound, Brandlight, Conductor, Peec Enterprise; SolCrys Custom from $2,000/month). The biggest change since May: Profound no longer lists $99 and $399 tiers; it offers a free 7-day trial and custom Enterprise pricing.

Direct answer

For one brand, about 50 prompts, 4 engines, and daily tracking, realistic list prices as of September 2026 are: Entry $29–$99/month (Otterly Lite $29, HubSpot AEO $50, Peec Starter $95, SolCrys Starter $99, Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit $99), mid-market $189–$495/month (Otterly Standard $189, Ahrefs Brand Radar AI Visibility Index $199, Peec Pro $245, Scrunch Core $250, SolCrys Pro $399, Peec Advanced $495), and enterprise, custom-quoted (Profound, Brandlight, Conductor, Peec Enterprise, and SolCrys Custom from $2,000/month). Entry tiers rarely cover 4 engines and 50 prompts on their own; engine and prompt add-ons are where the effective price climbs, as the math below shows.

The biggest change since we first published this page in May: Profound's $99 Starter and $399 Growth tiers are gone. Its pricing page now lists a free 7-day trial and a custom-priced Enterprise plan. Every price here was checked on the vendor's own pricing page on Sep 26, 2026.

Why AEO pricing is hard to compare

Three problems make AEO pricing pages hard to compare side by side without a worksheet:

Different units. Otterly prices by search prompts (15, 100, or 400) with Gemini, Google AI Mode, and Claude as paid add-ons. Peec prices by prompts and projects, with 3 models of your choice per plan and extra models as add-ons. Scrunch bundles custom prompts, seats, and page audits. Semrush sells a $99 toolkit license with 25 tracked prompts, plus $60/month per 50 extra prompts. Ahrefs sells Brand Radar by checks (1 prompt on 1 platform per update). HubSpot sells 25-prompt instances at $50 per brand. Profound quotes a custom Enterprise plan. SolCrys prices by tracked prompts, engines, and a monthly allowance of Deep Analyses and Content Audits. The same dollar amount buys very different scopes.

Different commitments. Most self-serve tiers discount annual billing by roughly 10–17%: HubSpot 10%, Otterly 15%, Peec about 15%, and Trakkr 17%. Enterprise tiers at Peec and others are billed annually.

Different scopes. A plan that measures where you stand is not the same product as a plan that helps you change it. Execution is no longer rare: Profound's Agents and AI Marketer, Writesonic, and Conductor generate content; Peec ranks recommended actions but says it doesn't write content; SolCrys turns diagnoses into human-approved tasks and re-verifies on a frozen prompt set. Compare on the total cost of the work delivered, not list price.

Per-engine cost-per-prompt math

Procurement teams shouldn't budget off list price. The honest unit is cost per prompt-engine run: what it costs to evaluate one prompt on one engine, at the cadence the vendor offers.

Worked example: 50 prompts × 4 engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini) × daily refresh = about 6,000 prompt-engine runs a month. Each row uses the vendor's cheapest published plan that covers that scope, with monthly list prices from the matrix above.

Profound isn't computable from public pricing. Semrush, Ahrefs, and HubSpot are left out because their published units don't map cleanly to this scope: HubSpot tracks 3 fixed engines, Semrush's toolkit documents ChatGPT and Google AI Mode for prompt tracking, and Ahrefs prices larger volumes in check packages without a public price.

How to read the table below. On cost per run, Peec is the cheapest fit for this exact scope, with Otterly close behind; Scrunch Core lands near Otterly per run but covers Copilot instead of Gemini. All 3 are built for measurement at scale. SolCrys costs more per run because the run isn't the whole product: Pro includes 40 Deep Analyses, 10 Content Audits, and Answer Accuracy on up to 15 prompts, the units that turn runs into approved changes. If you only want measurement, buy a measurement tool. If you want the diagnosis and the approved fix, cost per run is the wrong denominator; use cost per shipped fix.

Hidden cost callouts

These don't show up in the headline tier price, but they decide your 12-month cost:

Per-engine add-ons. Otterly charges $9–$149/month each for Google AI Mode and Gemini, and $29–$439/month for Claude, depending on tier. Peec charges $35, $85, or $165/month per extra model on Starter, Pro, or Advanced. Profound sets engine coverage in the Enterprise contract.

Seats, projects, and brands. Scrunch Core includes 5 user licenses and 1 brand workspace. Peec includes unlimited users but caps projects at 1, 2, or 5. Semrush charges $99 per additional toolkit user. HubSpot AEO is $50 per additional brand. SolCrys has no seat meter; its Agency plans are priced by client organization.

Units of diagnostic work. SolCrys caps Deep Analyses (1 on Free, 12 on Starter, 40 on Pro, 200 on Agency, 700 on Agency Pro) and Content Audits (1, 4, 10, 50, and 150) per month; Custom is scoped with you. Profound meters AI Marketer credits (limited on the trial, team-wide on Enterprise). Conductor lists Writing Assistant drafts per year (60 on Essentials, 180 on Growth) without public prices. Scrunch Core includes 5 site audits a month. Always ask what counts as a unit and how fast a team that ships will burn through it.

Integrations. Otterly includes API and MCP access from Standard. Peec's MCP server is on all paid plans, with API access and SSO listed under Enterprise. Profound lists API access and SSO/SAML on Enterprise. SolCrys includes MCP on paid plans (500 calls a month on Starter, 2.5K on Pro), not on Free; SSO (SAML/OIDC) can be scoped into an enterprise contract.

Setup. For any custom-quoted enterprise contract, ask whether prompt-set design and onboarding are billed separately, and get it in writing.

Cost vs ROI framing

A short version; the full treatment, including the spreadsheet a CFO will ask for, is in our AEO ROI business case.

Illustrative math, not a benchmark: a senior marketer running 50 prompts × 4 engines by hand (copy each prompt into ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini; record citations; tag mentions) might spend 8–12 hours per refresh. At a $75/hour blended cost, a weekly refresh is about $2,400–$3,600/month of human time before any analysis. At that workload:

  • A measurement-only tool at $30–$500/month pays for itself quickly, on time saved alone.
  • A measurement-plus-execution plan has to also save the hours of diagnosis, brief writing, prioritization, and re-test analysis your team would otherwise spend, or it's overspend.
  • An enterprise contract at several thousand dollars a month should also show movement on a frozen prompt set or in sourced pipeline over time (the harder case), or it's a brand-channel investment rather than an ROI calculation.

FAQ

Does Profound still have a $99 plan?

No. As of September 2026, Profound's pricing page lists 2 options: a free 7-day trial (50 prompts run daily on ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews, with limited AI Marketer credits) and a custom-priced Enterprise plan (up to 9 answer engines, SSO/SAML, and SOC 2). The $99 Starter and $399 Growth tiers it listed earlier are gone. We don't publish an estimated Enterprise price because we can't verify one; ask for a quote scoped to your prompts and engines. Brandlight and Conductor also don't publish prices.

What if I only want to track one engine?

Few plans are sold per engine anymore. The cheapest paid entry is Otterly Lite ($29/month, 15 prompts), which includes ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Copilot. HubSpot AEO ($50/month) covers 25 prompts on ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. SolCrys Free ($0) checks ChatGPT on demand on 10 prompts, and SolCrys Starter ($99/month) adds daily tracking on ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. If ChatGPT is all you need, start with a free baseline before paying for engines you won't use.

Is there a free AEO platform?

Not a full one. SolCrys Free ($0, email verification, no card) gives you 10 ChatGPT prompts checked on demand, 1 Deep Analysis, and 1 Content Audit a month. Mangools' AI Search Watcher Free tracks 5 prompts weekly. Everything else free is a one-time grader (HubSpot, Ahrefs, Semrush) or a trial (Profound for 7 days, Trakkr for 14 days with a card, HubSpot AEO, Scrunch, Otterly, and others). See Free ChatGPT Visibility Tools for the full list.

How does SolCrys price?

SolCrys has a Free $0 plan (10 ChatGPT prompts, 1 Deep Analysis, and 1 Content Audit, no credit card), Starter $99/month (20 prompts; ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews; 12 Deep Analyses and 4 Content Audits a month), and Pro $399/month (60 prompts; any 4 of ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Claude; 40 Deep Analyses, 10 Content Audits, and Answer Accuracy on up to 15 prompts). Agency ($1,499/month, 10 client organizations) and Agency Pro ($4,999/month, 25) serve client portfolios, and Custom starts at $2,000/month. MCP is included on paid plans; it's mostly read access, and an opt-in write permission lets an assistant mark an approved Action Hub task as published. It doesn't publish content to your site.

What about agency pricing across the category?

Agencies need multi-client workspaces, reporting, and per-client economics. Peec has dedicated agency plans priced in credits, with pitch workspaces. Otterly's Standard and Premium tiers include unlimited workspaces. Trakkr Scale ($500/month) covers 10 brands with white-label client portals. Profound's Agency Growth plan adds full client workspaces at $399/month each (100 prompts and 400 Agent credits a month) and includes 10 pitch workspaces a month; Agency Enterprise is custom. SolCrys Agency ($1,499/month) covers 10 client organizations and Agency Pro ($4,999/month) covers 25, each with 30 tracked prompts per client; white-label reporting is delivered as a service.

Are there annual billing discounts?

Yes, mostly in the 10–17% range for self-serve tiers as of September 2026: HubSpot AEO $50 to $45, Otterly 15% (Lite $29 to $25, Standard $189 to $160, Premium $489 to $422), Peec about 15% (Starter $95 to $80), and Trakkr 17%. Enterprise contracts are negotiated; always ask.

Why do per-prompt costs vary between vendors?

Because the product is different. In our worked example, measurement-first tools land around $0.02–$0.05 per prompt-engine run, and SolCrys Pro lands around $0.07 because the plan also includes the diagnosis and audit work that turns runs into approved changes. The denominator (runs) is the same; what's delivered isn't. Compare on cost per delivered unit of work, not cost per run.
