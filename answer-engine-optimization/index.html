AEO Fundamentals

Answer Engine Optimization: how brands become usable sources for AI answers

AEO is not a replacement for SEO. It is an operating layer that makes your brand easier for AI systems to retrieve, verify, cite, and summarize when users ask high-intent questions. The frameworks on this page are foundational - production AEO work should connect real prompt-intent data, engine-specific retrieval signals, and reviewable scoring instead of relying on a one-time checklist.

Updated 2026-05-04

Questions this guide answers

  • What is answer engine optimization?
  • How is AEO different from SEO?
  • What should a marketing team do first to improve AI search visibility?

Direct answer

Answer Engine Optimization is the practice of shaping your public web presence so AI answer engines can accurately retrieve your information, identify your expertise, cite your pages, and represent your brand in generated responses.

What AEO changes

Traditional SEO assumes a user scans a results page and chooses a link. AEO assumes an AI system may synthesize an answer first, using only the clearest and most trustworthy sources it can retrieve.

That changes the content job. Each important page must make the entity, answer, evidence, and next step obvious without relying on clever copy, hidden context, or JavaScript-only rendering.

  • Make the entity clear: product, company, category, audience, and use case.
  • Answer the question in the first screen, then support it with evidence.
  • Use headings, tables, FAQs, and schema to reduce ambiguity.
  • Keep claims current and easy to verify.

A practical AEO workflow

Start by building a prompt set around buyer questions, category comparisons, alternatives, risks, and implementation concerns. Measure how often the brand is mentioned and cited. Then map each weak answer pattern to a page that can become the strongest source for that topic.

  • Audit prompts across major answer engines.
  • Group prompts by intent and buying stage.
  • Identify missing citations, wrong facts, and weak comparisons.
  • Publish or refresh pages that answer those prompts directly.
  • Re-measure citation and answer accuracy after indexing.

Example prompt set

AEO work should start with prompts that mirror how buyers research, compare, and validate options. A small but useful first set usually includes category, competitor, risk, implementation, and brand-specific prompts.

  • Category: What are the best platforms for monitoring AI search visibility?
  • Comparison: How is answer engine optimization different from SEO?
  • Competitor: What are alternatives to an AI visibility monitoring platform?
  • Risk: How can a brand detect inaccurate AI answers about its product?
  • Brand: What does SolCrys do and who is it for?

FAQ

Is AEO the same as GEO?

They overlap. AEO is usually framed around answers and snippets, while GEO, or Generative Engine Optimization, is framed around generative AI retrieval, grounding, citations, and summaries.

Can schema alone improve AI visibility?

No. Schema helps classify content, but AI visibility also depends on crawlability, clear text, topical depth, evidence, authority, and whether the page actually answers the user's question.

What is the first AEO metric to track?

Start with citation rate and answer accuracy for a fixed set of high-intent prompts. Rankings alone do not show whether AI systems are using your brand as a source.
