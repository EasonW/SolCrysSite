What “AI visibility” means — and why your Google rank doesn’t cover it

When a buyer asks ChatGPT “what’s the best tool for X,” the answer names a short list of brands. AI visibility is whether you are on that list — decided by the citations and sources the model trusts, not by where you rank on Google. The tracker checks three outcomes for every prompt:

  • Mentioned — ChatGPT names your brand in the answer.
  • Cited — ChatGPT links to your page as a source.
  • Skipped — ChatGPT answers without you, often naming a rival.
