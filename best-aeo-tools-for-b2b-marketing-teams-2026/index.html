Why we wrote this guide differently

Most "best AEO tools" lists are opinion rankings written by SEO bloggers or vendor employees. Two problems with that:

  • They rarely check what they claim to compare. A list that calls a tool "best for AI visibility tracking" seldom says which engines, plans, or prices it looked at, or when.
  • They confuse the categories. Some vendors measure and recommend; others also route approved work and re-test it. Scoring them on one scale produces misleading rankings.

How we tried to correct for our own bias

We're SolCrys, and we sell an AEO platform, so treat this as a buyer-side comparison with a vendor-bias disclosure, not a neutral analyst report. To partly correct for that bias we did three things:

  • We verified every vendor fact on the vendor's own live pricing, product, or documentation pages on Sept. 26, 2026, and we say where a vendor's own pages disagree.
  • We compare published capabilities, not our own measurement of vendors. An earlier edition included a column on how often AI engines cited each vendor's website in our dataset; we removed it because a vendor's own citation share says little about what its product does for you.
  • For every column where we score ourselves favorably, we name the vendor for whom that column is also true.

How "best AEO platform for B2B" depends on three buying choices

There is no single "best"; the right tool depends on which question you're solving first. If you don't know yet, our AI Visibility Platform Buyer's Guide walks through 10 questions that surface the answer.

  • Dashboard or execution platform? A dashboard shows where your brand appears in AI answers and, increasingly, recommends what to do. An execution platform also turns the recommendation into approved work, helps produce it, and re-tests the same prompts afterward. A rough heuristic: if no one on your team owns shipping content changes each month, start with a dashboard.
  • Which AI engines matter for your buyers? ChatGPT has the largest audience (OpenAI reported more than 1 billion weekly users on Aug. 31, 2026), Google's AI Overviews reach more than 2.5 billion monthly users (Google, May 2026), and AI Mode passed 1 billion monthly users (Alphabet, July 2026). Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude matter for some buyer groups. The "monitors 50+ engines" pitch is mostly noise; what matters is the 2–4 engines your buyers actually use.
  • In-house, agency, or hybrid? Agencies need client separation, white-label reports, and per-client economics. In-house teams need depth on one brand. Hybrid teams need both, and many platforms force a hard choice.

How to read the matrix: five recommendation patterns

Below are five B2B buyer patterns and the vendor that fits each best. If your situation matches one, you have a starting point. Where we recommend ourselves, we name the alternative.

Best for enterprise B2B SaaS with category-leadership ambitions: Profound

Profound fits when the job is "convince the CMO, CEO, and board that AI search is a material brand channel, and fund it." Its enterprise narrative is the strongest in the category, its Enterprise plan covers up to 9 engines, and Agents now let a team draft and publish approved content from the same platform. Limitation: ongoing use means an Enterprise contract, and governance of what Agents publish is your team's to design.

If your team has the headcount to act on insights, this is the safest enterprise pick.

Best for mid-market B2B with a tight team and a real budget: Peec AI

Peec is fast to value: pricing is published, every plan includes unlimited users, and the dashboard is well organized. Actions (in beta) ranks what to fix with evidence and a suggested brief, and Peec can check AI answers against brand facts you set. Limitation: self-serve plans track 3 models before add-ons, and Actions stops at the recommendation; per Peec, it doesn't write the content.

Pick Peec if you have a 5–25-person marketing team and need answers in week 1, not month 2.

Best for B2B teams already on Ahrefs: Ahrefs Brand Radar

If you already pay for Ahrefs, Brand Radar is the lowest-friction add. Its index (449M+ prompts, per Ahrefs) lets you research any brand with no setup, citation data is accessible, and it sits next to Ahrefs' keyword and backlink tools. Limitation: there's no execution layer; Brand Radar is reporting infrastructure on an SEO stack. The same logic applies to Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit.

The right play for a B2B SEO lead who wants AI visibility in the existing SEO report, not a separate procurement cycle.

Best for B2B teams that have run an audit and need to act: SolCrys (with a caveat)

If the audit report has sat on a shelf for two months, you don't need another dashboard; you need the execution layer. SolCrys is built around all four steps, Measure → Diagnose → Execute → Verify. Five questions to ask in vendor calls

Use these to pressure-test what any vendor in this comparison tells you. For the longer 10-question version, see our AI Visibility Platform Buyer's Guide.

  • "Can you show me one URL-level citation for one of my competitors, right now, in your platform?" This tests whether the vendor captures cited URLs or only brand-mention strings.
  • "Which engines are included on the plan you're quoting, and what does each additional engine cost?" Several vendors include 3–4 engines and sell the rest as add-ons.
  • "Show me a recommendation your platform generated last week, and what happened to it." A pure dashboard cannot answer this.
  • "After we ship a fix, how do you show whether the answer moved?" Look for a rerun of the same prompts on the same engines, not a new prompt set.
  • "What's the source of your engine data: the consumer interface, an API, or synthetic prompts?" All three exist in this market, and API answers can differ from what buyers see.

FAQ

What's the difference between AEO, GEO, and AI SEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) describe the same work: improving how a brand shows up in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and others. "AI SEO" is an umbrella term some vendors use to bundle traditional SEO with AEO. For a B2B buyer, treat them as synonyms and evaluate vendors on capabilities, not naming. We cover the distinction in AEO vs SEO.

Do I need an AEO tool if my SEO traffic is fine?

It depends on the engine. For Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode, Google's May 2026 guide says optimizing for generative AI search is still SEO, so strong SEO carries over. ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Claude retrieve and cite sources differently, so a B2B brand can rank well in Google and still be missing from ChatGPT's answer to the same buyer question. If your buyers shortlist vendors in chatbots, you need to know what those engines say about you, and whom they recommend instead.

Which engines matter most for B2B?

No neutral study ranks engines by B2B buying influence yet. Start from reach and from your buyers: ChatGPT has the largest audience (more than 1 billion weekly users, per OpenAI in August 2026), Google's AI Overviews and AI Mode reach billions of monthly users, and Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude matter for some buyer groups. Ask a sample of recent buyers which ones they used, then focus depth on 3–5 engines. The "monitor 50+ engines" pitch usually means a long tail with little buyer use.

How long until an AEO platform shows results for a B2B team?

Plan for at least a quarter from setup to a readable trend on a tracked prompt set, assuming you act on the diagnoses. Measure visibility as a percentage across repeated runs, not a single answer: SparkToro's January 2026 study found less than a 1-in-1,000 chance that two runs return the same brand list in the same order. A dashboard's insights don't compound until your content team or agency acts on them. We cover the business case in AEO ROI: Building a CFO-Ready Business Case.

Should we build an AEO function in-house or hire an agency?

In-house wins on speed of iteration when your content team is strong; an agency wins on breadth of source-layer work (third-party coverage, reviews, community, and PR), which most in-house teams underweight. A hybrid, with an in-house platform license and an agency for source-layer work, is a common mid-market pattern.

Is "AI citation tracking" the same as "AI visibility monitoring"?

Not quite. AI visibility monitoring measures whether your brand was mentioned in an answer. AI citation tracking measures which URLs the engine pointed to as sources. Both matter for B2B: mention rate tells you about brand recall in the answer, and citation rate tells you about traffic potential and source-layer presence. We cover the distinction in ChatGPT Brand Mentions and Citation Gap Audit.
