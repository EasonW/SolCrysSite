What Answer Engine Optimization means

FAQ

What is Answer Engine Optimization?

Answer Engine Optimization, or AEO, is the practice of making a brand's expertise, products, and proof easy for AI systems and search engines to retrieve, understand, cite, and summarize accurately in generated answers.

How is AEO different from SEO?

SEO focuses on visibility in search result pages. AEO focuses on whether AI answer engines can use your content as a reliable source inside generated answers, comparisons, and recommendations.

Which AI surfaces should brands monitor?

Most brands should start with ChatGPT search, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot, Claude search, and any vertical AI assistant that influences research or buying decisions in their category.

Does SolCrys replace existing SEO work?

No. Strong technical SEO, useful content, crawlability, and authority are still the foundation. SolCrys extends that work into prompt-level AI visibility, citation readiness, and answer accuracy.
