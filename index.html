What Answer Engine Optimization means

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is the practice of making brand facts, proof, and pages easier for AI systems to retrieve, trust, cite, and summarize. SolCrys connects prompt-level measurement with crawlable, evidence-backed content strategy.

The SolCrys Loop: measure, diagnose, execute, verify

SolCrys closes the loop on AI search visibility. Each shipped action is tied to the same prompt set so teams can see which fixes actually changed the answer.

  1. Measure across engines. Run a fixed prompt set across ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews / AI Mode, and Perplexity on eligible plans. Capture mentions, citations, competitors, sentiment, and answer accuracy in one place.
  2. Diagnose the answer gap. Classify each weak answer as an absence, citation, accuracy, comparison, or action gap. Map each gap to the page or source most likely to fix it.
  3. Execute with Corporate Context. SolCrys uses your approved facts, claims, and guardrails to turn gaps into briefs, fix recommendations, and reviewable drafts your team can approve and ship.
  4. Verify and re-test. Re-run the same prompt set after the action ships. Track citation rate, answer accuracy, and recommendation share to prove which fixes actually moved the answer.

FAQ

What is AEO, and how is it different from SEO?

Answer Engine Optimization makes a brand's facts, proof, and pages easy for AI systems to retrieve, cite, and summarize. SEO targets the results page; AEO targets the answer itself.

Which AI surfaces should brands monitor?

Start with ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, and Perplexity. Claude, Copilot, and retail assistants are scoped add-ons where they materially shape the category.

How does SolCrys keep AI-generated content brand-safe?

Every action is grounded in your Corporate Context — approved facts, claims, and guardrails — and routes through human approval before it ships.

How does SolCrys prove a fix actually worked?

After an action ships, the same prompt set re-runs across the same engines — tying each fix to a measurable change in citation rate, answer accuracy, and recommendation share.
