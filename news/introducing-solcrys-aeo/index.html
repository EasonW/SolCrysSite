Introducing SolCrys AEO — the operating platform for brands to win AI search

Today we're launching SolCrys AEO. Measure visibility. Diagnose gaps. Activate the right actions. Verify what moved. All across leading answer engines, from one workspace governed by your brand.

Brand discovery is expanding beyond traditional search engines into AI search engines. Buyers now ask ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overview the questions they once typed into Google.

For marketing teams, the impact is immediate. CMOs are building AEO strategy. SEO teams have added AEO to their scope. Campaign teams want their sponsored thought-leadership articles to surface in AI engines.

But moving fast is hard. Teams are already using AI tools to draft content, SEO platforms to track rankings, and social listening tools to measure share of voice. None of these were built for AEO — and rolling out another tool across SEO, PMM, PR, brand, and communications can become a project of its own.

What teams want is simpler: one place to measure their brand across every AI answer engine, find the fixes that move the needle, and ship brand-consistent content.

Introducing SolCrys AEO

Today we're launching SolCrys AEO — the operating platform for brands to win AI search. Measure visibility. Diagnose gaps. Activate the right actions. Verify impact. All across leading answer engines, from one workspace governed by your brand tone and voice.

Industry-leading engine coverage

SolCrys covers the engines that matter most today: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overview, and Gemini. Additional engines, including shopping and retail, are available through selected plans.

Brand-safe and managed

SolCrys is fully managed. No infrastructure to stand up and maintain. The Corporate Context Layer keeps your messaging framework, brand writing guide, and approved claims governed and secured — forming the operating boundaries for every action SolCrys recommends.

One tool for every team and product line

Whether you're a lean team or a multi-product enterprise, SolCrys adapts to how your organization works. For smaller organizations, SolCrys brings the full AEO workflow into one focused workspace — so teams can move faster without adding operational complexity. For larger organizations, dedicated workspaces can be created by product line or function — each grounded in its own messaging, claims, and product truth.

Seamless workflow integration

SolCrys is built on the Model Context Protocol (MCP) — the open standard for connecting AI systems to the tools you already use. That means SolCrys plugs into your marketing stack natively: content management systems, AI writing and coding tools such as Claude Cowork, ChatGPT, and Codex. As more systems expose MCP servers, new integrations come online without custom integration work.

Product deep dive

Track the prompts that matter

You can't defend what you can't measure. SolCrys shows where your brand stands across leading answer engines — from mention rate and share of voice to citations and sentiment — tied to the prompts that matter most. Brand, PR, PMM, and campaign teams get one clear view your CMO can take to the board.

Find the gaps and ship the fixes

See which prompts your brand wins and where it's lagging. SolCrys identifies the top domains driving answers, shows where your brand is mentioned and why, then recommends actions to move the needle — from a content update or UGC strategy to a PR angle. Run content audits and manage the work in a kanban view with owners and status tracked across teams.

Verify what moved

After each action ships, re-run the same prompt set to measure what changed. SolCrys tracks citation rate, AI answer accuracy, recommendation share, and visibility movement, so teams can see which fixes improved the answer and which gaps still need work. Every result ties back to the original prompt, citation, and action — giving teams a clear way to prove impact.
