Introducing the SolCrys Corporate Context Engine

Answer Accuracy showed teams where AI gets their brand wrong. The Corporate Context Engine keeps the ground truth current — and Community Intelligence, our new product line for the communities shaping AI answers, brings that approved context to where buyers actually talk. Starting with Reddit.

By Gwen Chen, Co-Founder & CEO, SolCrys · LinkedIn

Last month, we launched SolCrys Answer Accuracy to help companies catch and correct what AI says about their brands. Today we're introducing the foundation underneath it — the Corporate Context Engine — along with Community Intelligence, a new product line that starts with Reddit.

Today's buyers don't search the way they used to. They ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overview, Claude, Perplexity, and other AI engines, and those answers increasingly determine which vendors make the shortlist. The challenge is that AI doesn't always have the latest facts. It may describe an outdated product, repeat inaccurate third-party claims, or miss your latest positioning altogether. Even if your website is current, AI may still rely on information published months or years ago.

SolCrys Answer Accuracy helps marketing teams stay ahead of those mistakes (see the launch announcement). It continuously monitors what AI engines say about your company and grades each answer against your approved company facts, flagging the claims that are inaccurate or unsupported — with the specific deviation and why it failed, so your team knows exactly which source to fix. For the first time, teams can measure the gap between what their company knows to be true and what AI tells their buyers, and every run that follows shows whether the fixes they shipped have started to land.

As customers adopted Answer Accuracy, they started asking a bigger question.

Accuracy isn't something you fix once

The problem wasn't correcting AI once — it was keeping AI current as the business evolved.

Customers launch products, update pricing, refine messaging, enter new markets, and respond to competitors. Each change affects what AI should say about their company, meaning an accurate answer today can become outdated tomorrow.

The approved information AI needs is rarely in one place. It's scattered across websites, documentation pages, launch announcements, and sales decks.

Answer Accuracy showed customers where AI was wrong. The next question became obvious: how do you continuously keep AI aligned with your business?

Introducing the Corporate Context Engine

Give SolCrys your website URL, and it builds a living record of your company. Instead of relying on scattered pages, presentations, and docs, your team gets a single source of truth for your products, messaging, market, and competitors.

As your business changes, your Corporate Context changes with it. Add a new deck, pricing update, messaging brief, or competitor announcement — a PDF, Word document, PowerPoint, Markdown file, or web page — and SolCrys reads the material, extracts the facts, and drafts the updates for your team to review. Nothing enters your approved context until a human says yes. Once approved, the latest context is available everywhere it's needed, with no need to re-brief every team or start over.

That context powers every workflow in SolCrys. Teams can understand why AI is getting their brand wrong, generate content grounded in approved messaging, and measure whether AI answers improve over time.

For teams whose ground truth reaches beyond what's published on the web, Managed Corporate Context remains available: our team builds and maintains the knowledge graph alongside yours. The Corporate Context Engine is the foundation everyone gets; Managed Corporate Context is the white-glove path onto it.

Several customers are already piloting the Corporate Context Engine to keep product updates, positioning changes, and other critical company knowledge current. Some measure whether new messaging is appearing in AI-generated answers. Others track how quickly AI stops mentioning a retired product after a major website redesign. One customer uses it to monitor how AI describes its products during a product recall, so buyers who ask an AI engine are pointed to the company's current published guidance rather than a stale third-party summary.

Introducing Community Intelligence, starting with Reddit

We're also launching SolCrys Community Intelligence, a new product line for the places buyers talk to each other. Communities, forums, and review sites are where opinions form long before a vendor makes anyone's shortlist — and they are increasingly what AI engines read when they answer questions about your category. A single well-upvoted thread can shape an AI answer more than a page of your own marketing site.

We're starting with Reddit, which has become one of the web's most influential sources of product research. Buyers ask questions, compare vendors, and share real-world experiences, and those conversations increasingly influence how AI answers similar questions. Over time, Community Intelligence will extend to the other places your market forms its opinions — Hacker News, G2, TrustRadius, and beyond.

Community Intelligence continuously monitors for discussions about your brand, your competitors, and your category. It surfaces feature requests, misconceptions, competitive comparisons, and the conversations that matter most — scored against the buyer prompts you already track.

When a discussion is worth joining, SolCrys drafts a response grounded in your approved Corporate Context — your product facts, messaging, and writing guide. Your team reviews it, edits it, and posts it themselves. SolCrys never posts on your behalf: the Reddit integration is read-only by design and has no ability to publish. We think that's the right boundary. Communities can tell when a brand shows up on autopilot, and automating your way in is the fastest way to lose standing in one.

The result is a closed loop: identify the conversations shaping AI, participate with accurate information, and measure how those changes influence future AI answers.

One foundation for every workflow

The Corporate Context Engine sits underneath the entire SolCrys platform. It captures the facts about your products, market, competitors, messaging, and writing guide, then powers Answer Accuracy, Deep Analysis, Recommended Actions, Community Intelligence, and every downstream workflow built on top of them.

This is just the beginning. Our vision is a Corporate Context Engine that becomes the foundation for every marketing workflow in SolCrys.
