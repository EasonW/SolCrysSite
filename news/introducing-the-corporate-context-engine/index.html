Announcing the SolCrys Corporate Context Engine

By Gwen Chen, Co-Founder & CEO, SolCrys · LinkedIn

A month ago, we launched SolCrys Answer Accuracy to help companies catch and correct what AI says about their brands.

Today's buyers don't search the way they used to. They ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overview, Claude, Perplexity, and other AI engines, and those answers increasingly determine which vendors make the shortlist. The challenge is that AI doesn't always have the latest facts. It may describe an outdated product, repeat inaccurate third-party claims, or miss your latest positioning altogether. Even if your website is current, AI may still rely on information published months or years ago.

SolCrys Answer Accuracy helps marketing teams stay ahead of those mistakes. It continuously monitors what AI engines say about your company, flags inaccurate or unsupported claims, recommends actions to correct them, and verifies whether those corrections actually change future AI answers. For the first time, teams can measure the gap between what their company knows to be true and what AI tells their buyers.

As customers adopted Answer Accuracy, they started asking a bigger question.

Accuracy isn't something you fix once

The problem wasn't correcting AI once—it was keeping AI current as the business evolved.

Customers launch products, update pricing, refine messaging, enter new markets, and respond to competitors. Each change affects what AI should say about their company, meaning an accurate answer today can become outdated tomorrow.

The approved information AI needs is rarely in one place. It's scattered across websites, documentation pages, launch announcements, and sales decks.

Answer Accuracy showed customers where AI was wrong. The next question became obvious: how do you continuously keep AI aligned with your business?

Introducing the Corporate Context Engine

Today, we're introducing the Corporate Context Engine.

Give SolCrys a product URL, and it builds a living record of your company. Instead of relying on scattered websites, presentations, or docs, your team gets a single source of truth for your products, messaging, market, and competitors.

As your business changes, your Corporate Context changes with it. Upload a new deck, pricing update, messaging brief, competitor announcement, or any PDF, PowerPoint, Word document, CSV, or image, and SolCrys refreshes that record automatically. There's no need to re-brief every team or start over. The latest approved context is available everywhere it is needed.

That context powers every workflow in SolCrys. Teams can understand why AI is getting their brand wrong, generate content grounded in approved messaging, and measure whether AI answers improve over time.

Several customers are already piloting the Corporate Context Engine. They're using it to keep product updates, positioning changes, and other critical company knowledge current. Some measure whether new messaging is appearing in AI-generated answers. Others track how quickly AI stops mentioning a retired product after a major website redesign. One customer uses the Engine to monitor how AI describes its products during a product recall, ensuring customers receive the latest approved information.

Introducing Community Intelligence

We're also launching SolCrys Community Intelligence, a new product line for the communities where buyers research, starting with Reddit.

Reddit has become one of the web's most influential sources of product research. Buyers ask questions, compare vendors, and share real-world experiences—and those conversations increasingly influence how AI answers similar questions.

Community Intelligence continuously monitors Reddit for discussions about your brand, products, competitors, and category. It identifies feature requests, misconceptions, competitive comparisons, and other conversations that matter most.

When a discussion needs attention, SolCrys drafts a response grounded in your approved Corporate Context—your product facts, messaging, and writing guide—so your team can respond quickly and consistently. You can publish the response as generated or review and edit it before posting, giving your team full control over every interaction.

The result is a closed loop: identify the conversations shaping AI, participate with accurate information, and measure how those changes influence future AI answers.

Reddit is where Community Intelligence starts, not where it stops. Over time it will extend to Hacker News, G2, TrustRadius, and the other communities that shape how buyers evaluate your category.

One foundation for every workflow

The Corporate Context Engine sits underneath the entire SolCrys platform. It captures the facts about your products, market, competitors, messaging, and writing guide, then powers Answer Accuracy, Deep Analysis, Recommended Actions, Community Intelligence, and every downstream workflow built on top of them.

This is just the beginning. Our vision is a Corporate Context Engine that becomes the foundation for every marketing workflow in SolCrys.

Answer Accuracy tells you where AI gets your brand wrong. The Corporate Context Engine keeps the ground truth current, so AI can keep pace as your business evolves.
