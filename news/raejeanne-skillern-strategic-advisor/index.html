SolCrys Names Raejeanne Skillern, Former AWS CMO and Intel Leader, as Strategic Advisor

Veteran tech executive joins SolCrys to help brands get discovered and recommended in AI search.

SolCrys, the AEO operating system for brands to win discovery in AI, today announced that Raejeanne Skillern — former Chief Marketing Officer at Amazon Web Services and a longtime hyperscale infrastructure leader — has joined the company as a strategic advisor.

Skillern brings more than 30 years of leadership across cloud, data center infrastructure, communications, and artificial intelligence. She has led multi-billion-dollar businesses at AWS, Intel, and Flex. Skillern currently serves on the boards of Jabil (NYSE: JBL) and Dycom Industries (NYSE: DY).

At AWS, Skillern led the global marketing organization, helping to modernize marketing for AI. At Flex, she ran a multi-billion P&L as President of the Communications, Enterprise & Cloud Division, repositioning the business for cloud, optical, and 5G. Over 25 years at Intel, she built the hyperscale data center business alongside the world's leading cloud providers.

About SolCrys

SolCrys was founded by a team of AI, data, search, marketing, and growth veterans from Amazon, Microsoft, Google, Alibaba, and Tencent, building the AEO operating system for the next era of brand discovery. The company today serves brands across HPC, AI, consumer goods, retail, and beyond.
