Direct answer

There are plenty of free ways to find out how visible your brand is in AI answers in September 2026. There is almost no free way to do anything about it. That gap is what this guide is about.

Sort the free options by the job and they fall into a 4-step loop: Measure (where do I stand in AI answers?) → Diagnose (what specifically is wrong?) → Execute (give me the exact change to ship) → Verify (re-test the same prompts to see whether it moved). We re-checked the free offerings of 16 vendors against those 4 steps on Sep 26, 2026. The result is still lopsided: every free tool measures, some diagnose, and among free plans that don't expire, only one hands you the change to ship. One free trial now reaches Execute too.

  • Verify is where every free option runs short, including ours. A re-test costs another run or another audit, so a complete free re-verify loop doesn't exist anywhere we looked.

The one distinction that decides everything: a score is not a fix

Most "free AEO tool" lists rank tools by how good the number is: how many engines, how fresh, how clean the dashboard. That is the wrong axis, because almost all of them live at the same step of the loop. They are scoreboards. A scoreboard tells you that you're losing 1–0. It doesn't tell you to change your striker.

The expensive, skilled, slow part of AEO is rarely the measurement. It's the work after: figuring out the specific change that will move a specific answer, writing it, shipping it, and checking whether it moved. So the distinction that changes your week is whether the free tool does any of that work for you, or hands the score back and wishes you luck.

That single question, score or fix, is what the comparison below sorts on.

The honest counters, handled

Two tools come closest to breaking the rule. Naming them is what makes this comparison trustworthy rather than convenient.

"But Writesonic's free LLM Optimizer rewrites my text"

It does. Writesonic's free LLM Optimizer adds expert quotes and authoritative citations to text you paste, and that is real execution of a kind.

It isn't loop execution, and the difference is the point. The optimizer is a standalone text box. It doesn't know which buyer prompt you're losing, it isn't fed by an audit of your live page, it doesn't emit the markup a crawler reads, and nothing ties the rewrite back to a re-test of the prompts that scored you. Writesonic's paid plans (from $79/mo billed annually) pair AI visibility tracking with AI article generation, with its Action Center fully included on Enterprise. On a free basis, it lands where everyone else does: a score on one screen, a generic rewriter on another, and a manual leap in between.

"But Profound's free trial runs Agents"

It does, and it's the one free trial we found that reaches Execute. As of September 2026, Profound's pricing page lists a free 7-day trial with 50 prompts run daily on ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews, plus Agents and a limited allotment of AI Marketer credits. That's a real way to see agent-drafted work before you buy.

It's still a trial. After 7 days, ongoing tracking and Agents move to Enterprise, which is custom-quoted, so it doesn't change the free-forever count. If you're already in an enterprise buying cycle, it's worth running.

Why the whole free category stops at the scoreboard

This isn't an accident of any one vendor; it's the business model. A free AI visibility score is an efficient lead magnet: it's cheap to run, it produces an alarming number ("you appear in 4% of answers"), and the alarm sells the paid plan. The recommendations, the drafting, the schema generation, and the re-test are the parts that cost real compute and reputation, and they're exactly what the paid tier is for. So the free experience is built to stop at the moment you'd want to act.

That logic is why "free AEO tool" so reliably means "free AEO grader." It's also why the one free plan that crosses into Execute is worth a closer look.

What "reaches Execute on free" actually looks like

On the SolCrys free plan, you run a Content Audit on a page (1 a month on free). For each finding, the audit returns the specific remediation: the JSON-LD block to paste, the heading or meta rewrite, the table or section to add, with the points each fix is worth. You promote a finding to an Action Hub task and you're holding the change, not a suggestion to go make one. Your team reviews and ships it; SolCrys never publishes to your site. That's the step other free plans leave to you.

The honest boundary, so we don't oversell our own column. Free is ChatGPT only, with on-demand checks on 10 prompts, up to 3 a month; Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Claude are paid. It includes 1 Content Audit and 1 Deep Analysis a month and 1 workspace. The re-audit that shows whether your score recovered is built and shipped, but it uses another monthly audit, so on free you get one clean pass through Measure → Diagnose → Execute, and Verify lands the next month or on a paid plan (Starter includes 4 Content Audits a month). A recovered audit score is an observation, not proof that the change moved AI answers. The free plan isn't the whole loop. It's the only free plan we found that gets you to the fix.

FAQ

Is there a free AEO tool that fixes my content, not just scores it?

Effectively one free plan, with caveats. Of the free offerings from 16 vendors we re-checked in September 2026, SolCrys is the only free plan that doesn't expire and reaches Execute: its free Content Audit hands you the concrete change (a JSON-LD block, a heading or meta rewrite) as an Action Hub task, tied to the page and prompts you were scored on. Profound's 7-day trial includes its Agents, but it's a trial. Writesonic's free LLM Optimizer rewrites pasted text, but it isn't connected to any measured gap. Everything else free stops at a score or a recommendation.

What is the best free AI visibility tracker?

For free ongoing tracking, Mangools AI Search Watcher Free: 5 prompts, weekly updates, no card. It's the only free-forever third-party tracker we found in September 2026; Trakkr now offers a 14-day trial with a card instead of a free plan. For a free one-shot score, HubSpot's AI Search Grader and Ahrefs' AI Visibility Checker are the most generous (no account). All of them measure; none drafts a fix.

Does Trakkr still have a free plan?

No. As of September 2026, Trakkr's pricing page lists a 14-day free trial that requires a card, then Growth at $100/mo (1 brand, 50 prompts, 8 models, daily) or Scale at $500/mo. It's a capable paid tracker, but it is no longer a free option.

Do any free AEO tools close the full loop (measure, diagnose, execute, verify)?

No, not on a free basis, including SolCrys. Verify (re-testing the same prompts or re-auditing the page to see whether a change moved the number) costs another run everywhere it exists. SolCrys gets furthest on a free plan: measure, diagnose, and the fix, with the re-audit landing the next month or on a paid plan. Paid execution platforms, including SolCrys, Profound, and AirOps (see our closed-loop scorecard), put execution and verification on paid plans.

Why is everything free just a "grader"?

Because a free score is this category's lead magnet. It's cheap to produce, it surfaces an alarming number, and the alarm sells the paid plan. The recommendations, drafting, schema generation, and re-test are the costly parts the paid tier exists to sell, so the free experience is built to stop right before you'd act.
