Why Gemini needs its own tracker

Gemini is not "ChatGPT, but from Google." Three structural differences mean a tracker that aggregates Gemini into a single AI-visibility number misses the answer your buyers see:

  • Account-aware retrieval. Gemini can read the signed-in user's Google account context (recent Search queries, opened emails, Drive content if granted). The same prompt from two buyers returns two materially different answers, especially in B2B where account context is rich. Mention rate per prompt is a population statistic — useful but coarser than ChatGPT.
  • Three surfaces, three behaviors. Gemini consumer (free + Advanced), Gemini API, and Workspace Gemini use different system prompts and different retrieval depths. The same buyer query in your category can return your brand on one surface and your competitor on the other.
  • Tight Google Search integration. Gemini blends Google Search results into answer construction more aggressively than ChatGPT or Perplexity. The pages that rank #1-3 on Google for your category prompts disproportionately drive Gemini mention rate.
  • Multimodal context. Gemini handles attached PDFs, images, and Workspace docs natively. Buyers comparing vendors often upload a vendor PDF and ask Gemini to compare — visibility is then a function of what's in the uploaded document, not just public web content.

What a Gemini visibility tracker measures

Four signals, measured across both the consumer chatbot and the API surface:

  • Mention rate (population-level). Across N category prompts, how often does Gemini name your brand on a fresh, unauthenticated session? This is the baseline; account-aware answers will trend differently per user.
  • Cross-surface delta. Does Gemini consumer mention your brand more often than Gemini API on the same prompt? A 15+ point gap signals that the consumer system prompt is doing work you don't see at the API layer.
  • Search-result borrow rate. What percentage of Gemini's answer content traces back to pages already ranking on Google Search? High borrow rate = your existing SEO directly fuels Gemini visibility; low borrow rate = Gemini is leaning on training-time knowledge and you need different content levers.
  • Recency sensitivity. Are recent (<90 days) pages on your domain more visible to Gemini than older ones? Gemini is meaningfully more recency-weighted than ChatGPT, especially after the AI Overviews integration.

Gemini consumer vs. API vs. Workspace — which to track

Most teams should track at minimum the consumer surface (gemini.google.com) because that's where buyer research happens. B2B SaaS and API-integrated categories should additionally track the Gemini API surface, because most enterprise AI features built on Gemini route through the API — your competitor's product that uses Gemini in their app is calling the API, not the consumer chatbot. Workspace Gemini is the late-stage surface where deals get reviewed, and tracking it requires Workspace Enterprise.

FAQ

What is a Gemini visibility tracker?

A Gemini visibility tracker is a tool that runs a fixed set of buyer prompts through Google Gemini and reports how often Gemini names your brand, which competitors it names instead, and how its answers vary across Gemini's three surfaces — consumer chatbot, Gemini API, and Workspace embedding. Because Gemini is account-aware, the tracker reports population-level mention rate (fresh unauthenticated sessions) rather than per-user numbers.

How is Gemini different from ChatGPT for brand visibility?

Four ways. Gemini reads the signed-in user's Google account context (account-aware retrieval), so the same prompt returns different answers per user. Gemini blends Google Search results into answers more aggressively, so your SEO rank disproportionately drives Gemini mention rate. Gemini exposes three surfaces (consumer, API, Workspace) with different behaviors. And Gemini is materially more recency-weighted, especially after AI Overviews integration.

Can I track Gemini API separately from the consumer chatbot?

Yes, on the SolCrys Brand plan. The API surface uses different system prompts and lighter retrieval scaffolding than the consumer chatbot, so the same buyer prompt can return materially different brand mention rates. Tracking both separately is important if your buyers' workflow tools (Notion AI, Workspace add-ons, custom enterprise assistants) use the Gemini API under the hood — that's the actual buyer surface for enterprise B2B.

Does Gemini Advanced (Ultra) show different answers than free Gemini?

Yes, especially on technical comparison queries. Gemini Advanced (Ultra) has deeper reasoning chains and different retrieval depth than the free tier. The same prompt can return materially different brand mention rates between tiers. SolCrys tracks both tiers separately on the Brand plan and reports the delta so you can spot tier-specific visibility patterns.

How does Google AI Mode relate to Gemini tracking?

Google AI Mode is the conversational answer surface inside Google Search (powered by Gemini under the hood). Tracking AI Mode is structurally closer to tracking AI Overviews than tracking the Gemini chatbot — it's SERP-integrated, query-driven, and reflects organic search rank more than chatbot-style retrieval.
