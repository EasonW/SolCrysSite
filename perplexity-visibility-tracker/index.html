Direct answer

A Perplexity visibility tracker measures two things on a fixed set of buyer prompts, run repeatedly: how often Perplexity names your brand in the answer (mention rate), and how often it cites your domain, a competitor's, or a third party as a source (citation share). Perplexity shows its sources with the answer, so readers can click through, and the source list is where most fix work starts.

How Perplexity builds an answer, and why it changes what you track

Perplexity is retrieval-first: it searches the web when the question is asked, writes the answer from the pages it retrieved, and lists those pages as numbered sources. Four consequences for tracking:

  • Sources come with every answer. Per Perplexity's help center, every answer includes citations linking to the original sources. And with Perplexity's ad tests phased out (reported in February 2026 by Search Engine Land), organic citation is the only way into the answer, so citation share is a first-class metric.
  • Answers move with the web. Because retrieval happens at query time, a competitor's new comparison page or a fresh review can change the answer within days. Repeated runs on a schedule tell you more than a one-time check.
  • Two crawlers, two jobs. Per Perplexity's crawler documentation, PerplexityBot surfaces and links websites in Perplexity's results, while Perplexity-User fetches a page when a user's question calls for it and generally ignores robots.txt because a user requested it. Blocking PerplexityBot, in robots.txt or at your CDN, keeps you out of its results.
  • Subscribers can pick the model. Perplexity Pro and Max subscribers can choose among models from several labs, including OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic models alongside Perplexity's own Sonar; free users can't (Perplexity). The same prompt can read differently from one account to the next, so treat a tracker's number as a baseline, not every buyer's screen.

What a Perplexity visibility tracker should measure

Four signals, on the same buyer-intent prompts, run repeatedly. Repetition matters: SparkToro's January 2026 study of 2,961 runs found the same brand list in the same order less than once in 1,000 runs, and endorsed visibility percentage across repeated runs, not ranking position, as the metric (SparkToro).

  • Mention rate. Across repeated runs of your prompt set, how often does Perplexity name your brand in the answer?
  • Citation share. Of the sources Perplexity cites for those prompts, how many are your pages, a competitor's, or third-party editorial and community pages?
  • Cited but not named. Does Perplexity cite a page that mentions you, then leave you out of the answer? That gap is usually fixable on the page itself.
  • Favorability. When Perplexity names you, is it a recommendation, a neutral listing, or a caveat? Being named last with a warning is not the same as being recommended.

Mention rate vs. citation share: reading the four combinations

On a Perplexity answer to "best CRM for small B2B teams," your brand can appear in the prose without your page in the sources, or the reverse. Each combination points to a different fix:

  • Named, not cited. Perplexity names you, but the sources it lists are other people's pages; third-party coverage is carrying you. Lever: make your own page the clearest answer to that prompt, and keep the third-party pages accurate.
  • Cited, not named. Perplexity retrieves a page that mentions you but leaves you out of the answer. Lever: make sure the cited page states plainly who you are, what category you're in, and who you're for. The Mentions You filter in Citations helps find these.
  • Both. Keep it: re-run the same prompts to confirm it holds, and watch which competitor pages enter the source list.
  • Neither. The most common starting state. Lever: look at which domains Perplexity cites for the prompt. Those pages are the source layer you need to be in, through your own content, earned coverage, or both.

What tends to move Perplexity visibility

Perplexity cites pages it can retrieve and quote, so the levers are retrieval hygiene and source-layer work, not tricks:

  • Let PerplexityBot in. Check robots.txt and your CDN's bot settings. Cloudflare began blocking AI crawlers by default for new domains on July 1, 2025, and split its controls into search, agent, and training crawlers on July 1, 2026; check which you block.
  • Answer at the passage level. Perplexity retrieves passages, so a page where each section answers one question in its opening sentences is easier to quote than one that buries the answer.
  • Keep facts current and dated. Retrieval happens at query time, so a stale pricing page or comparison gets quoted as-is.
  • Earn the third-party pages Perplexity already cites. If the source list for your prompts is review sites, trade press, or forums, that's where your presence has to be. Community sources behave differently by engine: ChatGPT all but stopped citing Reddit in the week of Aug. 10, 2026 (Search Engine Journal), while Perplexity was largely unaffected.

FAQ

What is a Perplexity visibility tracker?

A tool that runs a fixed set of buyer prompts through Perplexity on a schedule and reports your mention rate (how often Perplexity names you in the answer) and your citation share (how often it cites your domain versus competitors and third parties). Because Perplexity lists its sources with each answer, citation share is directly measurable there.

Is Perplexity included in the SolCrys free workspace?

No. The free workspace tracks ChatGPT only: 10 prompts and 1 content audit, with email verification and no credit card. Starter ($99/mo) adds Gemini and Google AI Overviews. Perplexity is on Pro ($399/mo), as one of any 4 of 5 engines, and on Custom, which runs all 5.

How is Perplexity tracking different from ChatGPT tracking?

Perplexity retrieves from the web for each question and lists its sources, so which pages it cites matters as much as whether it names you. ChatGPT searches the web when it decides a question needs it and otherwise answers from what the model already knows. Track both on the same prompts and read them separately, not as one blended number.

Does SolCrys track Perplexity Pro model choices separately?

No. SolCrys measures one consistent Perplexity configuration per workspace, so change over time is comparable. It does not split results by the model a paid subscriber selects. If your buyers rely on a specific model, spot-check those answers by hand and compare them with the tracked baseline.

What's the most important metric for Perplexity visibility?

Citation share, read alongside mention rate. Perplexity's source list is where readers click and where most fixes start, but a page can be cited without your brand being named. Read both, then check how favorably the answer treats you (the Recommendation Score in SolCrys).
