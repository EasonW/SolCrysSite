Pricing FAQ

What counts as a tracked prompt?

One prompt × one AI engine × one daily run = one tracked prompt. A prompt monitored daily on ChatGPT and Perplexity counts as two tracked prompts. You can pause, resume, and swap prompts any time without overage charges.

What counts as a brand or workspace?

A workspace is one brand and its variants — for example, a parent brand plus up to 5 sub-brands or product lines. Distinct brands, business units, or geographies belong in separate workspaces. Agencies use one workspace per client organization.

Which AI engines are included?

Every plan includes all six: ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, and Google AI Overviews. We do not paywall engines or charge per-LLM add-ons. New engines are added at no extra cost.

What's the difference between For Brands and For Agencies?

Same SolCrys platform, two ways to buy. For Brands gives a dedicated prompt allocation per workspace — predictable for in-house marketing teams. For Agencies gives a shared prompt pool that you can reallocate across client organizations as needs change, plus pitch projects (free 7-day prospect trials) and white-label deliverables.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Monthly plans cancel any time and stop billing at the next cycle. Annual plans can be cancelled, with the unused portion refunded prorated, no questions asked.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes — when our self-serve product opens, every Brand plan includes a 14-day free trial with full access. Today, we offer a free AI Visibility Audit so you can see a sample report and decide before joining the trial list.

Do you support invoice, wire, or PO payment?

Yes, on Brand Scale, all Agency plans, and Enterprise. Smaller plans are billed by credit card via Stripe.

Is my data private? Do you train models on my data?

Never. Your prompts, brand context, and audit results are private and are never used to train any model. SOC 2 Type II audit is in progress, and we are GDPR compliant. Enterprise plans include a custom DPA and your choice of region.

Can I switch between monthly and annual?

Yes. Switch from monthly to annual any time and we apply the discount at the upgrade. Switch from annual to monthly at the next renewal.

Do you offer a startup or non-profit discount?

Yes. Verified seed-stage startups (under $5M raised, under 25 employees) and registered non-profits get 30% off any Brand plan for the first year. Apply via the contact form below.
